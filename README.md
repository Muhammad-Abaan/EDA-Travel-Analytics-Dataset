# Travel Dataset EDA  
 
This repository contains an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of a travel and tourism dataset. The dataset includes customer demographics, travel preferences, product pitches, and engagement details. The analysis highlights customer behavior, sales patterns, and potential business insights.  

## Dataset Description  
The dataset consists of the following key features:  
- **CustomerID** – Unique identifier for each customer  
- **ProdTaken** – Whether the product was purchased  
- **Age** – Customer age  
- **TypeofContact** – Marketing contact type  
- **CityTier** – Customer’s city tier  
- **DurationOfPitch** – Duration of sales pitch  
- **Occupation** – Occupation of customer  
- **Gender** – Gender of customer  
- **NumberOfPersonVisiting** – Number of people traveling  
- **NumberOfFollowups** – Follow-up interactions made  
- **ProductPitched** – Product type pitched  
- **PreferredPropertyStar** – Customer’s preferred hotel star rating  
- **MaritalStatus** – Marital status of customer  
- **NumberOfTrips** – Number of trips taken  
- **Passport** – Possession of passport  
- **PitchSatisfactionScore** – Satisfaction score of sales pitch  
- **OwnCar** – Car ownership  
- **NumberOfChildrenVisiting** – Children accompanying customer  
- **Designation** – Job designation of customer  
- **MonthlyIncome** – Monthly income of customer
